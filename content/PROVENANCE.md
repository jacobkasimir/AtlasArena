# Content provenance

- 160 original, template-authored production questions: 40 countries × 4 question families.
- Families: capitals, textual flag identification, currencies, and regional true/false.
- Four production difficulty tiers, 10 countries per tier.
- Separate public demo: 12 different countries × 4 question families = 48 questions.
- No proprietary quiz database was copied.
- No external image or boundary assets are included.
- The original globe illustration and app icons are created by this project.
- Flag descriptions are text, providing a nonvisual alternative and avoiding platform-dependent flag emoji.
